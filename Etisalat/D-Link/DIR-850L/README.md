# ⚠️ FIRMWARE COMPATIBILITY NOTICE

> **IMPORTANT:** Check the label on the bottom of your router before updating.

---

### ✅ Supported Hardware
* **Model:** D-Link DIR-850L
* **Hardware Revision:** `HW B1` (ET / ISP Edition)

---

### ❌ Incompatible Hardware
* **Hardware Revisions:** All other revisions (A1, A2, etc.)

---

> 🚨 **WARNING:** This ISP firmware is **strictly compatible with HW B1**. Do **NOT** attempt to flash this on any other hardware version, as it will **brick** your device.
